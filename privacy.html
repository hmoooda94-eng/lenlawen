Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

"Lenlawen Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "لنلوّن معاً يا أطفال" (Lenlawen Maan) to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set video details and thumbnails, and read basic information about the owner's own channel. It does not access any other user's data.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. Access tokens are stored privately and used only to upload the channel's videos.

Children

The channel's videos are made for children and are marked as "made for kids" on YouTube. We do not collect any information from children who watch the videos. Viewing on YouTube is subject to YouTube's own policies.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
